Cotton that gets a second life

Recycled cotton fiber is made by shredding cotton textile waste, such as cutting scraps and leftover fabric, back into fiber. Spinners turn it into yarn, and it also goes into non-woven products and filling. It keeps waste out of landfill and reduces the need for new raw material.

How recycled cotton fiber is made

From leftover cotton to fiber that is ready to spin.

  1. Cotton waste

    Cutting scraps and leftover fabric.

  2. Sorting

    Separated by material and colour.

  3. Shredding

    Pulled back into loose fiber.

  4. Baling

    Pressed and strapped for shipping.

  5. Second life

    Yarn, non-woven products and filling.
